## Move the least

Good progressions usually come from smooth inner motion, not from clever chord names.

Keep common tones, move everything else by step, and let the bass carry the leaps.

### Try this

Harmonise a descending scale in the top voice with four different chords per note. Most of
your options will sound fine — the ones that sound *inevitable* are the ones where the
inner voices barely moved.
