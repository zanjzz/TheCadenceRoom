## Same tonic, different neighbourhood

Modal interchange means keeping your tonic and borrowing a chord from a parallel mode.

In C major, `Ab`, `Fm`, and `Eb` all arrive from C minor. They sound dramatic because the
tonic never moved — only the colour around it did.

### Rule of thumb

Borrow late. A borrowed chord in bar one is a key change; in bar seven it is a surprise.
