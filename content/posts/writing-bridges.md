## A bridge answers a question

If the verse and chorus already said everything, the bridge has nothing to do.

Common ways to shift vantage point:

- Start on a chord you have avoided so far.
- Borrow the relative minor.
- Pedal one bass note under moving upper voices.
- Drop the rhythm section entirely for two bars.

Pick one. Two is usually a new song.
