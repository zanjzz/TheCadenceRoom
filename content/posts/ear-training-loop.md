## Hear function, not names

Naming a chord is trivia. Hearing what it wants to do next is musicianship.

### The loop

- Two minutes: sing the tonic over random chords.
- Four minutes: identify I, IV, V, vi only.
- Four minutes: transcribe one bar of a song by ear.

Do it daily for a month. It compounds faster than any lesson.
