## Register first, EQ second

Most muddy keyboard mixes are arrangement problems wearing an EQ costume.

Decide who owns 200-500 Hz. Usually it is not the pad.

### A quick pass

1. High-pass the pad until the chord still reads.
2. Give the piano a narrow dip where the vocal sits.
3. Check in mono. If the harmony disappears, the voicing is too wide.
