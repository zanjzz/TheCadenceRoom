## Passing chords are punctuation

They do not change where the song is going. They change how it feels getting there.

Start with `F/A -> F#dim -> C/G`. One chromatic step in the bass, one new colour, and the
congregation hears movement without hearing theory.

### A practical drill

- Play a hymn with block chords only.
- Add one diminished passing chord per phrase.
- Remove any that fights the melody.

Restraint is the skill. The chord you leave out is the one people remember.
